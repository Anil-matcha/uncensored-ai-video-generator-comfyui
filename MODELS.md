# Which spicy video model should I pick?

A short guide to the unrestricted video models you can run from ComfyUI through the [Muapi node pack](https://github.com/SamurAIGPT/muapi-comfyui). Resolutions are from Muapi's endpoint definitions (October 2026). Check the live Muapi pages for current pricing.

## Models that start from text

- **Seedance 2.5 Spicy** goes up to 4K, and Muapi also has edit and extend endpoints for it. A good pick when you want the final version of a clip.
- **Wan 3.0 Spicy** goes up to 1080p and has a sound toggle.
- **Seedance 2 Spicy** also reaches 4K. It has a fast version for when you are in a hurry.
- **Seedance 2 Mini Spicy** is the lighter, cheaper version (480p and 720p). A good pick for testing a prompt before you spend on a bigger render.

## Models that start from your own picture

- **MiniMax H3 Spicy** brings a picture to life at 480p or 768p, for 3 to 15 seconds.
- **Wan 2.7 Spicy** and **Wan 2.6 Spicy** do the same at 720p or 1080p.
- **Wan 2.2 Spicy** is the older, quick option for testing ideas (480p or 720p, 5 or 8 seconds).

## A simple way to work

1. Test a prompt cheaply with Seedance 2 Mini Spicy.
2. Move up to Seedance 2.5 Spicy (or Wan 3.0 Spicy) for the version you want to keep.
